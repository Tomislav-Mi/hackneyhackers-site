Privacy Policy — SideTill

Effective date: 26 October 2026

SideTill is an app for sole traders and side hustles: you log a job, send the invoice, record the money that comes in and goes out, and see what you really earned. This policy explains, in plain words, what the app does and does not do with information. The short version: your records stay on your phone. We run no servers, we have no accounts, and we cannot see your clients, invoices or money. What does leave the phone is described under "What uses the internet" below: your subscription, through Google Play and RevenueCat; diagnostics that Google's on-device tools send to Google; and, only if you turn it on, a backup to your own cloud drive.

What the app stores on your phone

Everything below lives only in the app's private storage on your device. We have no copy and no way to read it.

  • About you: your name, address and a phone number or email address, which go on your invoices because an invoice must say who it is from; a trading name if you have one; your trade; your currency; how you take payment and any payment details you type in (for example bank details or a PayPal address), printed on your invoices.
  • Your clients: the names, phone numbers, email addresses, addresses and notes you enter.
  • Your work and money: services and prices, invoices, the hours you log, payments received, expenses, and the reminders ("nudges") you send.
  • Receipt photos you take or choose, kept with the expense they belong to.
  • Settings: your invoice numbering and notes, whether you want reminders, and the app's own bookkeeping (for example when you first opened it, and whether it has shown you the one-time offer).

Deleting it: uninstalling the app, or clearing its storage in Android settings, permanently deletes all of it. If you turned on automatic backup, the backup in your own cloud drive stays there until you delete it (see below).

Things you send yourself

When you share an invoice (as a PDF or as text), send a nudge, or export your records for your accountant, Android's share sheet opens and you choose where it goes: email, a messaging app, a file, a printer. SideTill hands the document to the app you pick and sends nothing anywhere itself.

The camera, photos and microphone

  • Camera and photos: used only when you photograph or choose a receipt. The total, date and shop are read from the photo on the phone, by Google's ML Kit text recognition, which is built into the app. The photo is not uploaded to be read.
  • Microphone: used only while you tap to speak a note. Your speech is turned into text on the phone, by Android's own on-device speech recognition. Your voice is not sent to us or to anyone. If you never tap the microphone, it is never used.

What uses the internet

  1. Your subscription: Google Play and RevenueCat. SideTill Pro is a subscription sold through Google Play. Payment details go to Google, never to us. To know whether you have Pro, the app uses RevenueCat, a subscription service. RevenueCat receives an ID created at random for this installation of the app (no name, no account), your purchase and subscription status, your phone's model and brand, its Android version, the app's language, your store country, and RevenueCat's own diagnostics. It never receives your clients, invoices, amounts or anything else you record. RevenueCat's privacy policy is at https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy; Google's governs the purchase itself.
  2. Diagnostics from Google's ML Kit. Reading receipts uses Google's ML Kit on the phone. ML Kit sends Google technical information about itself, such as this app's package name, the ML Kit version and an ID created at random for this installation, for diagnostics and usage analytics. It never includes your photos or anything you record. It is Google's, not ours; we receive none of it. Google describes it at https://developers.google.com/ml-kit/android-data-disclosure.
  3. Automatic backup, only if you turn it on. SideTill Pro can keep a copy of your records and receipt photos in your own Google Drive (on iPhone, your own iCloud Drive). On Google Drive it signs in with your Google account and uses a private SideTill folder that only SideTill can open; it cannot see your other files. The copy goes from your phone to your drive; it never passes through us, and we cannot read it. The app keeps the Google account's email address on the phone to show you where your backup goes. Turning backup off stops it; the copy already in your drive stays until you delete it there.
  4. Android's own device backup. If you use Android's "Back up to Google", Android may copy the app's records (not its receipt photos) to your Google account, as it does for other apps. That is Android's, under Google's terms, and you control it in Android settings.

What the app does not do

  • No accounts and no sign-in, except signing in to your own Google Drive if you turn on backup there.
  • No analytics of ours, no trackers, no advertising and no ad SDKs.
  • No selling or sharing of personal data, and nothing sent to us: we have no server to receive it.
  • Notifications: optional reminders the day after an invoice is due, a weekly note while anything is outstanding, and a monthly nudge to back up. They are created on your phone and shown only if you allow them; a reminder that names a client or an amount is hidden on a locked phone until you unlock it.

Children

SideTill is made for adults running a business and is not directed at children under 13.

Changes to this policy

If the app ever starts doing something this policy does not cover, the policy will be updated first and the effective date above will change.
