Bigger than You

On Google Play

How big is a blue whale, really? Guess the size of everything, then see the truth side by side.

A size comparison game about the scale of everything, from a proton to the edge of the universe. Slide to guess, zoom in on the real size, and see how you measure up.

Free. No ads. Works offline.

Get it on Google Play

Ways to play

  • Guess the scale. Slide to guess how big something is, then zoom in on the real size. The closer you get, the more points.
  • Bigger or smaller. Is the next thing bigger or smaller than this one? Keep the streak going.
  • Sort the lineup. Put five things in order, smallest to largest.
  • Scale yourself. How many of you tall is a giraffe? Guess sizes against your own height.
  • Daily challenge. The same five rounds for everyone, every day.

In the app

What is inside

  • Three worlds of sizes. Earth, from a flea to Mount Everest. Space, from moons to galaxies and the universe itself. Micro, from a grain of pollen down to a proton. Over 3,100 objects, each with a drawing and a fact to read once you know the answer.
  • Three games about countries. Resize a country until it is as big as it really is, choose which has the higher figure, or rank a handful by population, area and GDP. 155 countries, with figures from the World Bank, the UN and Wikipedia.
  • Compare at true scale. Pick up to eight things, yourself included, and stand them side by side. Share the comparison as a picture.
  • Your own objects. Add things with a name and a size, and send a set to friends as a message. They can play it as a quiz before adding it.
  • Challenge a friend. Send the same rounds with your score to beat. It goes as an ordinary message.
  • Achievements and leaderboards. 20 achievements, plus the daily challenge and best streaks, with Google Play Games.
  • Nine languages. English, German, French, Spanish, Portuguese, Japanese, Korean, Vietnamese and Indonesian, following your phone's language.

The price

Free to download, with no ads and no subscription. Earth and the countries are free to play in every mode. Space and Micro are a one-time unlock, and Google Play shows you the price in your own currency before you buy.

No account needed, and the game works with no signal at all.
