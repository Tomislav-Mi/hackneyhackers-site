Pause & Pray

An Android app for Christians who want their phone to interrupt them less. Choose the apps that pull at you, and each time one opens, Pause & Pray first offers a short prayer or a verse of scripture — then steps out of the way. Everything stays on the phone; there are no accounts, no ads and no tracking.

Pause & Prose

The same idea, for people who mean to read more than they do. Choose the apps that pull at you, and each time one opens, Pause & Prose first offers one great line of writing — Austen, Dickinson, Marcus Aurelius, Wilde — then steps out of the way. Or work through a whole book a passage at a time. Every word is built into the app, so a pause opens instantly and works with no signal at all.

World of Pomos

A Pomodoro timer where every work sequence is a roguelike descent. Each focus interval fuels the lantern that carries you deeper into the Endless Library, past the Shadows barring the stairs. Finish the sequence and you extract with the haul; stop early and you leave empty-handed. The work you did is never lost. Only the treasure is.
