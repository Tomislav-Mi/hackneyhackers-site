Hackney Hackers

About us

Hackney Hackers is a small software studio in Hackney, London. We build useful apps — software that respects the person holding the phone.

What we do

We make apps that ask for a moment of your attention and then give it back: Pause & Prose, Unwind, Bigger than You, SideTill and, in development, World of Pomos.

Wherever we can, the work happens on your phone, so the apps keep working with no signal and your data stays with you.
