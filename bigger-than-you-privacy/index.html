Privacy Policy — Bigger than You

Effective date: 12 October 2026

Bigger than You (called „Größer als du“ in German and “Maior que Você” in Brazilian Portuguese) is a size comparison game for Android by Hackney Hackers Ltd. This page explains what the app does with your information. The short version: nothing leaves your phone unless you send it yourself.

What we collect

Nothing. The app has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. It doesn't have permission to use the internet, so it can't send anything to us or to anyone else.

What stays on your phone

To work, the app keeps a few things in its own private storage on your device:

  • Your settings: theme, level, world, units and the kinds of objects you play with.
  • Your best scores and streaks, and the day you last played the daily challenge.
  • The height you give it, so you can compare things with yourself.
  • What's on your Compare stage.
  • Objects you make yourself: their names, sizes and drawings, and a cropped copy of any photo you choose for one.

We can't see any of this. You can delete it at any time by clearing the app's storage in your phone's settings, or by uninstalling the app.

Photos

When you give one of your own objects a photo, you choose it with Android's photo picker. The app only sees the picture you pick, not the rest of your gallery, and keeps a cropped copy in its own storage. Photos are never included in the sets you share.

Sharing

You can share a comparison picture, a set of your own objects or a challenge. The app hands it to Android's share menu and you choose where it goes. From there, the app you pick, such as your messaging or email app, handles it under its own privacy policy. A comparison picture shows whatever is on your stage, including photos of your own objects if you've put them there.

Backups

If backup is switched on on your phone, Android may copy the app's data into your device backup in your Google account, so a new phone can restore it. Google handles that under your backup settings and its own privacy policy; we can't see it.

Google Play

Google Play gives us anonymous, combined statistics, such as how many times the app has been installed, and crash reports from people who have chosen to share diagnostics with Google. These come from Google, not from the app, and don't identify you.

Children

The game is meant for players aged 13 and over. It collects no personal information from anyone, children included.

Changes to this policy

If the app ever starts doing something this policy doesn't cover, the policy will be updated first and the effective date above will change.
