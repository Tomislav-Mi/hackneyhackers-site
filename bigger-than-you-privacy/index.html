Privacy Policy — Bigger than You

Effective date: 5 November 2026

Bigger than You (called „Größer als du“ in German, “Maior que Você” in Brazilian Portuguese, 「キミより大きい」 in Japanese, “Más grande que tú” in Spanish, “나보다 클까?” in Korean, “To Đùng Nhỏ Xíu” in Vietnamese, “Plus grand que toi” in French, “Raksasa Mungil” in Indonesian, “Boyundan Büyük” in Turkish, “कितना बड़ा?” in Hindi, “A occhio” in Italian, «بالعين المجردة» in Arabic, „Ile to ma?” in Polish, “ใหญ่แค่ไหน?” in Thai and 「目測王」 in Traditional Chinese) is a size comparison game for Android by Hackney Hackers Ltd. This page explains what the app does with your information. The short version: we collect nothing. Your achievements and scores go to Google Play Games only if you use it, and purchases are handled by Google Play.

What we collect

Nothing. The app has no accounts of its own, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. It doesn't have permission to use the internet, so it can't send anything to us. The only things the app sends from your phone go through two Google services on it, which are described below: Google Play Games, if you use it, and Google Play, if you buy the unlock.

What stays on your phone

To work, the app keeps a few things in its own private storage on your device:

  • Your settings: theme, level, world, units, the kinds of objects you play with, and the statistics and way of answering you choose for the country games.
  • Your best scores and streaks, the days you've played each daily and your run of days, and which friends' challenges and sets you've already played.
  • Your collection: each object you've guessed, how often, your best points for it and how far off your guesses were, which the game uses to show your sense of scale.
  • Your experience points and rank.
  • Whether the daily reminder is on, and its time.
  • Your achievements, and any scores waiting to go to Play Games, plus how many games you've finished, the day of your first and the day the game last asked Google Play to show its rating card.
  • Whether you've unlocked Pro, so it stays open when you're offline.
  • The height you give it, so you can compare things with yourself.
  • What's on your Compare stage.
  • Objects you make yourself: their names, sizes and drawings, and a cropped copy of any photo you choose for one.

We can't see any of this. You can delete it at any time by clearing the app's storage in your phone's settings, or by uninstalling the app.

Notifications

The daily reminder is off until you turn it on. When it's on, the game sets an alarm on your phone and shows at most one notification a day, only if you haven't played the daily challenge or the Countries daily yet. On Android 13 and up, Android asks you first whether the game may show notifications. The game is also allowed to start when your phone restarts, the game is updated, or its clock or time zone changes, but only to set that alarm again. Nothing is sent anywhere. You can turn the reminder off in the game's settings, or block its notifications in Android's.

Photos

When you give one of your own objects a photo, you choose it with Android's photo picker. The app only sees the picture you pick, not the rest of your gallery, and keeps a cropped copy in its own storage. Photos are never included in the sets you share.

Sharing

You can share a comparison picture, a set of your own objects or a challenge. The app hands it to Android's share menu and you choose where it goes. From there, the app you pick, such as your messaging or email app, handles it under its own privacy policy. A comparison picture shows whatever is on your stage, including photos of your own objects if you've put them there.

Backups

If backup is switched on on your phone, Android may copy the app's data into your device backup in your Google account, so a new phone can restore it. Google handles that under your backup settings and its own privacy policy; we can't see it.

Google Play Games

If you're signed in to Google Play Games on your phone, the game sends it the achievements you earn and your scores for the daily challenge and your best streaks in Bigger or smaller and Higher or lower. It does this through the Play Games app on your phone, not over the internet itself. Play Games shows them on your Play Games profile and on leaderboards, under your Play Games name and picture, to the people your Play Games settings allow. When you sign in, Play Games tells the game your player name and picture, which the game doesn't keep. Google handles all of this under its own privacy policy, and you can delete it from your Play Games profile. You don't need Play Games to play: without it, everything works the same and nothing is sent.

Purchases

Bigger than You Pro (Space and Micro, a Space and a Micro daily, past dailies to play again, your sense of scale by category and ranks beyond Everest) is a one-time purchase through Google Play. Google takes the payment; we never see your card or payment details. Google gives us the order details it gives every seller, such as the order number, the date, the price and the country, and the game asks Google Play on your phone whether you own the unlock.

Rating the game

Now and then, after a game that went well, the app may ask Google Play to show its rating card. Google Play decides whether it appears. Anything you write or rate there goes to Google Play, not to the app, and is published under Google Play's own rules.

Google Play

Google Play gives us anonymous, combined statistics, such as how many times the app has been installed, and crash reports from people who have chosen to share diagnostics with Google. These come from Google, not from the app, and don't identify you.

Wikipedia

A fact card can link to the object's article on Wikipedia. If you tap it, your phone's browser opens the page, and Wikipedia sees that visit as it would any other, under its own privacy policy. The app itself sends nothing.

Children

The game is meant for players aged 13 and over. We collect no personal information from anyone, children included.

Changes to this policy

If the app ever starts doing something this policy doesn't cover, the policy will be updated first and the effective date above will change.
